Training section

A section page is the contents page for that section: what it covers, what you will be able to do, and the lessons in order. The rail is on the left, breadcrumbs above, and the same components as everywhere else in between.

Head — the number IS the hero

The section used to announce itself three times over the same two lines: a breadcrumb, a small numbered disc under it, and a // TRAINING SECTION kicker beside that. The breadcrumb already says where you are, so the chip and the kicker are gone and the number became the hero's GROUND — set at the full height of the band, anchored to the left edge, drawn into the background rather than written on top of it. It is scenery, not a label: you read the title, and the number is the size of the place you are standing in.

It is an <svg> rather than a font-size, because “as tall as the hero” is a relationship CSS cannot express when the hero's height comes from its own content. preserveAspectRatio states it exactly, at any content length and any viewport, with no clamp to keep in sync. Low fill, slightly stronger stroke, paint-order: stroke fill so the outline keeps an even weight instead of the fill eating half of it from inside.

It sits WHOLE in the frame and .ns-hero--numbered indents the copy past it, so the number and the title share the band rather than stacking. An earlier version ran the digit off the left edge — at that size a crop reads as a rendering fault, not as a decision. aria-hidden, since the section is already named twice above it.
