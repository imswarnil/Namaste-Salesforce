Section navigation

The rail that runs down every training and lesson page — and the answer to the rule you set: the section you are in stays open, every other one is closed.

How the behaviour works

Each section is a <details> sharing one name attribute. That is a native HTML exclusive accordion: the browser itself keeps exactly one open and closes the previous when another is opened. No JavaScript, no flash on load, and it still behaves if the script never runs. A browser without exclusive-details support just allows more than one open — which degrades correctly rather than breaking.

Open section 3 below and section 2 closes on its own.
