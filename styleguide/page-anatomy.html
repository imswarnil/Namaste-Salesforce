Pages

Page anatomy

The standing pages — about, contact, terms, privacy, 404 — are neither collections nor posts, so they share their own small shell: head, body, optional sticky rail, closing action. Get this right once and every informational page on the site is the same job.

The shell

.ns-page → __inner → __head (kicker, title, lede, meta) → __main (__body + optional __aside) → __foot. Layouts: --narrow for a single reading column, --rail for prose plus a contents rail, --rail-start when the contents should lead.

Narrow — the default for most pages
