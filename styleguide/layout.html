Layout & grid

How a page is measured: twelve columns, a gutter that scales with the viewport, and shells that are one column below lg and split above it.

The grid

TokenValue
--grid-columns
--grid-gutter
--grid-gutter-sm
--grid-gutter-lg
--grid-min
--grid-min-sm
--grid-min-lg

Twelve columns, because 12 divides by 2, 3, 4 and 6 — every layout the site needs falls out of it. The default .ns-grid reflows on CONTENT (auto-fit + --grid-min) rather than at a breakpoint you guessed.

Breakpoints

Tailwind's defaults, documented so custom media queries never invent a new one: sm 640 · md 768 · lg 1024 · xl 1280 · 2xl 1536. lg is the important one — below it the site is a single column with drawers, at and above it rails and sidebars are permanent.
