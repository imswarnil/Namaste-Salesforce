Borders & radii

The hairline IS the structuring device. Geometry is sharp and specific — not “12px and pills everywhere”.

Widths

Radii

--radius-card
--radius-btn
--radius-sm
--radius-pill

Cards 6px, buttons and inputs 4px. --radius-pill is reserved for TRUE pills — tags, pager controls, avatars — never the default button or card.
