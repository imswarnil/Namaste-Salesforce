Colour

One working blue carries every interactive meaning. Everything else is a surface, an ink, or a status — and status never becomes a background wash.

--color-brand-100

--color-brand-500 is #0176D3, the one signal colour. 600 is the hover step; 900 is the navy that grounds dark mode and every hero.

Worth knowing: accent is currently a near-duplicate of brand — --color-accent-500 is the same #0176d3. Consistent with “one signal colour”, but it does mean the --accent variants read almost identically to the default. Either give accent a genuinely different hue, or drop those variants.

Semantic roles
