FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

Do I need a Salesforce licence to follow along?

No. Every lesson uses a free Developer Edition org. The first lesson of Admin Foundations walks you through signing up for one.

Is this enough to pass the Administrator certification?

The free courses cover the concepts. The member quizzes are written in the exam's style, and the training trail maps to the exam outline section by section.

How is this different from Trailhead?

Trailhead teaches the clicks. This site teaches the business underneath them — sales cycles, lead routing, quote-to-cash — and then shows the platform features that serve them. Do both.

Can I read the source of a lesson?

Yes. The theme, the design system and the demo content are open source on GitHub. Fork the platform if you want to teach something else with it.

What happens if I cancel membership?

You keep every deck and cheat sheet you downloaded, and the free courses stay free. Your progress is saved if you come back.

Why this exists

I went looking for the map. It was not there.

I started in Salesforce CRM Analytics, building dashboards for teams I did not fully understand. At Twilio I met the word GTM for the first time — pipeline, opportunity management, quote-to-cash — whole functions I had been reporting on without knowing how they worked.

So I am learning Salesforce properly, with the business underneath it, and writing every step down here. If it works, this becomes the one place a step-by-step GTM learning path lives. If it does not, you still get the notes.
