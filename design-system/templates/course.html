Salesforce Admin Foundations

Objects, fields, page layouts, users and the setup menu — the ground floor of every org, taught in the order a real admin meets it. Twelve lessons, one free Developer Edition, no prior Salesforce.

Beginner 12 lessons 3 h 10 min Updated Sep 2026 Free
Start lesson 1 Preview the syllabus SSSwarnil SinghaiInstructor
2:14 intro

Most "admin" courses teach the clicks. This one teaches the reasons — why an object exists, what a field promises, who a profile is for — and then shows the clicks that follow. By the end you can read an org you did not build and explain it to someone who does not use Salesforce.

Everything runs in a free Developer Edition. Lesson one gets you one.

What you will learn

  • What an org is, and the difference between editions, sandboxes and scratch orgs
  • Objects, fields and records — and the API-name rule that never bends
  • Choosing between lookup, master-detail and junction relationships
  • Users, profiles, permission sets and the sharing model, top down
  • Page layouts vs Lightning record pages — one page per audience
  • A record-triggered Flow with a fault path, built and debugged live

Who it is for

Career changers, analysts who inherited an org, and developers who skipped the admin part. No Salesforce experience needed; if you have used a spreadsheet, you have the prerequisites.

Before you start

Sign up for a Developer Edition org (free, lesson 1 shows how) and keep it open in a second tab. Every lesson ends with a five-minute exercise in it.

Is the whole course free?

Ten of twelve lessons are free. The two members-only lessons at the end of Module 3 cover fault paths and a production-readiness quiz.

How long does it take?

Three hours of lessons, roughly six with the exercises. Most people finish in two weeks at a lesson a day.

Does it map to the Administrator certification?

It covers the concepts behind every exam section. The member quizzes are written in the exam's style; the training trail lists the mapping section by section.

Can I get a certificate?

Yes — complete all twelve lessons and the quiz and a certificate appears on your profile, with a link you can put on LinkedIn.
