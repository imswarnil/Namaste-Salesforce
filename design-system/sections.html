Before you start

Do I need a Salesforce licence?

No. Every lesson uses a free Developer Edition org; lesson one shows you how to get one.

Is this enough to pass the Administrator exam?

The free courses cover the concepts; the member quizzes are written in the exam's style.

How is this different from Trailhead?

Trailhead teaches the clicks. This teaches the business underneath them, then the clicks. Do both.

Can I read the source of a lesson?

Yes — theme, design system and demo content are open source.
